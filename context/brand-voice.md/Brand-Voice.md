Forecast Accuracy & Forecast Structure
John and Karl have initiated a review of the forecast process and structure. The room forecast is very accurate. However, certain calculated Key Business Indicators (KBIs) lack the use of active ones or feature duplicate KBIs in their formulas. For instance, the calculation of the KBI for available guests' breakfast, uses "#8005 – zBqt Grp Bkfst (w/ OS)" KBI. This setup applies to available guest calculations for all meal periods, not just breakfast. There appears to be duplication of KBIs in formula #5950.

Moreover, the revenue center forecast is currently not being generated, only being put in manually. We propose rectifying available guest KBIs and subsequently utilizing the system generated forecast. Additionally, it's imperative to reassess and adjust seating capacities and operational hours for all revenue center outlets. Catering frequently experiences delays in updating Delphi with precise covers and beverage revenue, along with communicating pop-ups effectively.

Forecast
We started our review of forecast accuracy and discussed forecasting methodologies with managers from rooms, outlets, and parking. Rooms forecasting is based on 14 day distributed from revenue management. The initial review demonstrates a very accurate forecast when not under construction. A few challenges arose once construction started on the Victorian building. Revenue management started an upsell program which has caused slight fluctuations in forecast accuracy. Arrival forecast accuracy shows that many days are over-forecasted. This might be due to Victorian renovation since there is a high guest demand to stay in that building. 

 

 

Outlet forecasts are initially done outside of UniFocus and compared with adjustments made in UniFocus if necessary. Managers are not very confident of UniFocus-produced forecasts. Further analysis of KBI configuration is needed to determine why the system-produced forecasts are inaccurate. Similarly, parking forecasts are conducted outside of UniFocus because the system does not capture all the data for scheduling.
